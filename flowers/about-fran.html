Meet Francine

The heart, hands, and vision behind the bouquets

Our Founder's History

Francine Miller didn't just want to build a flower business—she wanted to build an anchor point for community connection in the Mission District.

Moving to San Francisco in the early 1990s, Francine quickly fell in love with the vibrant local culture of Valencia Street. Believing that accessible natural beauty can spark genuine daily joy, she opened the doors to Fran's Flowers in 1995 with nothing but local wholesale buds and an open heart.

Rooted in Giving Back

To Francine, supporting the community means more than crafting exceptional design. Having witnessed structural challenges in the Bay Area, she established our cornerstone mandate on day one: allocating exactly ten percent of total storefront earnings directly to local organizations combating housing instability and homelessness across the neighborhood.

Today, she can still be found in the store every morning at dawn, selecting fresh stems, mentoring aspiring local floral designers, and ensuring kindness remains our most important crop.
